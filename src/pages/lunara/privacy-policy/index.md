# Privacy Policy

## General
Except for data collected by Apple, such as when you have enabled Share with Developers, I do not collect any data.
No personal information, usage data or any other information is collected — nothing.

## iCloud
I use iCloud for syncing your troupes, games and collection, with all synced data securely stored in your own iCloud account.

## Card data updates
The app periodically downloads publicly available game card data from GitHub to stay up to date.
This request contains no personal information whatsoever.

## Camera
The camera is only used to scan troupe share QR codes, entirely on your device.
No images are stored or transmitted.

## Third-party services
This policy does not apply to third-party services you use here.
You can consult the relevant privacy policies on the websites of any services you are using.
