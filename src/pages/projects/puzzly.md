Puzzly is a collection of bite-sized daily logic puzzles for iPhone and iPad, built with Swift & SwiftUI.

Every game generates its puzzles procedurally, and each puzzle is guaranteed to have exactly one solution. A new daily puzzle appears in every game each day — the same board for every player worldwide — alongside unlimited practice puzzles, streaks, and best times.

The games:

- **Queens** — place one crown in every row, column, and color region
- **Tango** — balance suns and moons under equality constraints
- **Zip** — draw one path that fills the whole board in number order
- **Mini Sudoku** — 6×6 sudoku with 2×3 boxes
- **Patches** — cover the board with rectangles matching size and shape clues
- **Wend** — find four words winding through a walled grid
- **Pixel** — picture-logic nonograms, always solvable without guessing
- **Lumen** — a lights-out puzzle with par scoring
- **Bones** — place dominoes to satisfy region conditions
- **Harbor** — connect islands with bridges
