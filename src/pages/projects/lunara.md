Application i built using Swift & SwiftUI.

Lunara is an unofficial, free companion for Moonstone by Goblin King Games: a full card
compendium that keeps itself up to date, a troupe builder with upgrade cards and QR
sharing, a live game tracker for health, energy, moonstones and statuses, plus melee
matchup and arcane odds calculators.
