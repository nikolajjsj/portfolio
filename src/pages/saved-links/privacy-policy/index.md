## General
Except for data collected by Apple, such as when you have enabled Share with Developers, I do not collect any data.
No personal information, usage data or any other information is collected — nothing.
There is no account to create and no server of mine that your links are sent to.

## iCloud
I use iCloud for syncing your links and tags, with all synced data securely stored in your own iCloud account.

## Reading articles
When you save a link, the app fetches that page and prepares the reader view entirely on your device.
The page is requested directly from the website, exactly as your browser would request it, so that site
can see the request — but your list of saved links is never sent anywhere, and no third party processes it.

## Site icons
For sites that do not provide an icon of their own, the app asks Google's public favicon service for one.
Only the site's domain name is sent, never the full address of the page you saved.

## Third-party services
This policy does not apply to third-party services you use here.
You can consult the relevant privacy policies on the websites of any services you are using.
